# Code of conduct

carpool follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and assume good faith. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't
accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report a problem, open a private report through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability")
and mark it as a conduct report, or contact the maintainer listed on the repository profile. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that break these rules and may block repeat
offenders.
